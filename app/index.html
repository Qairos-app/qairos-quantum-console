    How Qairos works

    The Qairos Moment is the first time an armed canary key breaks. It is recorded on-chain and every module reacts to it.

    1 · Canary

    Bounties on elliptic-curve keys. Breaking an armed key proves quantum capability.

    2 · Oracle

    Each armed break raises threatLevel() by one, on-chain.

    3 · Guard

    Contracts using QairosGuard lock themselves at their threshold.

    4 · Ark

    Owners recover funds with a post-quantum key committed before the Qairos Moment.

    5 · Markets

    Predictions and insurance settle from the oracle automatically.

    Once the breaker trips, QairosVault only releases funds through a two-step recovery that reveals your pre-Moment key. A quantum attacker cannot know it.

    3. Send step 2, wait at least one block, then send step 3 to move the balance to a fresh address.
